Hannah has spent nine years reporting on health research for consumer audiences,
with the last four focused almost entirely on the first thousand days. She is
particularly interested in the gap between what paediatric research actually
shows and what parents are told — a gap that tends to be widest on the topics
that cause the most anxiety.

Before joining The Baby Insider she wrote about clinical research for a national
health title, where she covered the evidence base behind infant sleep advice.
She works closely with our medical review team and does not publish clinical
guidance without a clinician's sign-off.

She lives in Dublin with her partner and two children, and has personally
survived two sleep regressions.
