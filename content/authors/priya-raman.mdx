Dr Raman is a board-certified paediatrician who has practised general
paediatrics for eleven years, with a clinical interest in newborn care and safe
sleep counselling.

On The Baby Insider she reviews articles covering clinical topics — anything
that touches on symptoms, safety, medication or developmental concern — and
checks that our guidance reflects current professional recommendations, that
uncertainty is described honestly, and that we are clear about when a parent
should speak to their own clinician rather than read another article.

Medical review means checking accuracy and clinical safety. It is not an
endorsement of any product mentioned, and our reviewers have no financial
interest in the products we cover.
