The Baby Insider is free to read. This page explains how it is paid for.

## How we make money

**Affiliate commissions.** Some links to products are affiliate links. If you
buy through one, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you. These
links are marked in the page code as sponsored, and any article containing them
carries a disclosure near the top — not buried at the bottom.

**Advertising.** We display advertising on some pages. Ad slots are labelled
"Advertisement". Advertisers have no input into editorial content and no
advance sight of it.

**Our own product.** See below.

## Ownership: the disclosure that matters most

**The Baby Insider is published by Omega Solutions, the company that also makes
the BabyLeap app.**

That means when we write about BabyLeap, we are writing about our own product
and we profit directly if you subscribe. This is the largest conflict of
interest on this site.

We handle it like this:

- Every article mentioning BabyLeap carries an ownership disclosure at the top.
  This is enforced by our publishing system, not by memory — an article that
  mentions the product without the disclosure fails to build.
- Clinical claims about it are reviewed by a clinician with no stake in it.
- Articles recommending it name the competing products we would recommend
  instead, and say who they are better for.
- Our [evaluation criteria](/how-we-evaluate) are published in advance and
  applied to our own product exactly as they are to anyone else's.

You should discount our coverage of BabyLeap accordingly. We would rather tell
you that plainly than have you find out later.

## What a commercial relationship does not buy

- A recommendation
- A rank position
- Removal of criticism
- Advance approval of an article
- Exemption from our safety criteria

If a product cannot meet the criteria on our
[evaluation page](/how-we-evaluate), no commercial arrangement changes that.

## Your data

Advertising and analytics partners may set cookies. You can manage this through
our [cookie settings](/legal/cookie-settings), and our
[privacy policy](/legal/privacy) explains what is collected.
