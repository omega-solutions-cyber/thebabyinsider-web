## What cookies are

Small files stored by your browser that let a site remember things between
visits.

## Categories we use

**Strictly necessary.** Required for the site to function — security and basic
preferences. These cannot be switched off.

**Analytics.** Help us understand which articles are read and where people get
stuck, so we can improve them. Aggregated.

**Advertising.** Used to measure and, in some cases, personalise advertising.
See our [advertising disclosure](/advertising-disclosure).

## Managing cookies

Set your preferences at [cookie settings](/legal/cookie-settings), or block and
delete cookies in your browser. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break
parts of the site.

## Third parties

Our analytics and advertising partners may set their own cookies, governed by
their own policies. See our [privacy policy](/legal/privacy) for who they are.
