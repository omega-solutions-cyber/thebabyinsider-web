## Editorial and corrections

[editorial@thebabyinsider.com](mailto:editorial@thebabyinsider.com)

If you have spotted an error — particularly a clinical one — this is the fastest
route. We prioritise reports of inaccuracy in health content and will correct
openly, with the change noted on the article.

Please include the article URL and, where you can, the source you believe is
correct.

## Medical questions

We cannot answer questions about your baby's health, and it would be unsafe for
us to try. We do not know your child, cannot examine them, and are not your
clinician.

Please contact your paediatrician, GP or local urgent care service. If you think
your child needs medical attention now, seek it immediately rather than waiting
for a reply from anyone.
