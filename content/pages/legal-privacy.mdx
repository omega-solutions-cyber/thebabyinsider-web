## What we collect

**Information you give us.** Your email address if you subscribe to our
newsletter, and anything you include when you contact us.

**Information collected automatically.** Standard analytics — pages viewed,
approximate location derived from IP, device and browser type, and referring
site.

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, health information about you or
your child.

## Why we process it

- To send the newsletter you asked for (consent — withdrawable at any time)
- To understand which articles are useful and improve them (legitimate interest)
- To operate and secure the site (legitimate interest)

## Who we share it with

Service providers acting on our instructions:

- **Klaviyo** — newsletter delivery
- **Vercel** — hosting and performance measurement
- **PostHog** — product analytics

We do not sell your personal information.

## Cookies

We use cookies for essential site function, analytics and advertising. Manage
your preferences at [cookie settings](/legal/cookie-settings); see our
[cookie policy](/legal/cookie-policy) for the detail.

## Retention

Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is
retained on a rolling basis.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete
or port your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Every
newsletter email includes a one-click unsubscribe link.

To exercise any of these rights, email
[hello@thebabyinsider.com](mailto:hello@thebabyinsider.com).

## Children

This site is for parents and carers. It is not directed at children and we do
not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

Material changes will be posted here with an updated date.
