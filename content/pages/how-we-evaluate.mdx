We partner with brands in the parenting and baby-care industry and recommend
products on this site. Those relationships are commercial, and we think you
should be able to see exactly what standards a product had to meet before it
appeared here.

## Selection criteria

Before a product or brand is considered for coverage, we look at:

- Reputation and credibility of the product and the brand
- The company's track record and how long it has been operating
- Any product recalls, safety notices or regulatory action
- Any controversies involving the company or its products
- Publicly available information, including independent testing where it exists
- Whether the product is appropriate for the age group we would recommend it for

## Our evaluation process

Every product and brand goes through the same assessment, regardless of whether
there is a commercial relationship attached:

- **Safety first.** For anything used with or near an infant, we check for
  recalls, applicable safety standards, and alignment with current paediatric
  safe-sleep and child-safety guidance. Products that conflict with that
  guidance are not recommended, at any price, under any commercial arrangement.
- **Claims must be supportable.** Health and developmental claims are checked
  against the evidence. Where a claim is marketing rather than science, we say
  so in the article.
- **Compliance.** We check for relevant certifications, applicable standards,
  and any FDA, FTC or equivalent warnings.
- **Honest framing.** We do not recommend products that work by manufacturing
  parental anxiety, and we do not present developmental ranges as deadlines.
- **Clinical review.** Anything with a health claim is reviewed by a member of
  our [medical review team](/medical-review-team) before publication.

## Rankings and commercial relationships

Commercial relationships do not determine rank order. Our evaluation criteria
are set before testing begins, and the drawbacks we find are published alongside
the strengths — including for products we have a financial interest in.

Where a commercial relationship exists, it is disclosed in the article itself,
not only here. See our [advertising disclosure](/advertising-disclosure) for how
those relationships work.


## Removal and review

We re-check recommended products periodically. A product is removed from our
recommendations if it:

- Is subject to a safety recall or regulatory warning
- Changes its claims in a way we cannot support
- Is involved in practices we consider misleading to parents
- No longer meets the criteria above

We aim to act quickly, but we cannot always act immediately, and not every brand
mentioned on this site has been through the full evaluation process. Where that
is the case, the article will say so.

## Telling us we got it wrong

If you think a recommendation here is unsafe, out of date, or wrong, email
[editorial@thebabyinsider.com](mailto:editorial@thebabyinsider.com). Corrections
are made openly, with the change noted on the article.
